# How to Use Draftroom

## Quick Start Guide

### 1️⃣ You'll See These Default Furniture Items in the Left Sidebar:
- Sofa (2.0 × 0.9 m)
- Armchair (0.8 × 0.8 m)
- Coffee Table (1.1 × 0.55 m)
- Dining Table (1.4 × 0.9 m)
- Dining Chair (0.45 × 0.45 m)
- Bed (1.6 × 2.0 m)
- Bookshelf (0.9 × 0.3 m)
- Rug (2.0 × 1.4 m)
- Plant (0.4 × 0.4 m)
- Floor Lamp (0.35 × 0.35 m)
- TV Stand (1.2 × 0.4 m)
- Desk (1.2 × 0.6 m)

### 2️⃣ Drag Furniture to Place It
1. Look at the **left sidebar** - you'll see cards for each furniture item with a small icon and dimensions
2. **Click and drag** a furniture item from the sidebar onto the floor (the grid area)
3. As you drag, you'll see a semi-transparent preview and dimension lines
4. Drop it by releasing the mouse/touch

### 3️⃣ Move Furniture Around
1. **Click on placed furniture** to select it (it will highlight)
2. **Drag it** to a new position
3. The status bar shows which item is selected

### 4️⃣ Rotate Furniture
Select a furniture item, then either:
- Click the **rotation buttons** (← →) in the status bar
- Or press **`[`** and **`]`** keys to rotate in 15° increments

### 5️⃣ Resize Furniture
1. **Select a furniture item** by clicking on it
2. Click **"Resize selected"** button (bottom left)
3. Enter new **Width** and **Depth** in meters
4. Click **"Resize"** to apply

### 6️⃣ Delete Furniture
Select furniture and then:
- Click the **trash icon** (🗑️) in the status bar, OR
- Press **Delete** or **Backspace** key

### 7️⃣ Change Room Dimensions
At the top of the page, adjust:
- **Width** and **Depth** inputs (in meters)
- The room will resize immediately
- Any furniture that no longer fits will be repositioned

### 8️⃣ Save a Room Layout
1. Arrange furniture how you like
2. Click **"Save layout"** (top right header)
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Living Room Setup")
4. Click **"Add Furniture"** to save it to the database

### 9️⃣ Load a Saved Layout
1. Click the **dropdown** in the header
2. Select a layout you previously saved
3. The entire room, furniture, and dimensions load instantly

### 🔟 Switch Between 3D and Plan View
- **3D View** (default): Perspective view - right-click drag to orbit around
- **Plan View**: Top-down view - perfect for precise placement - middle-click drag to pan

### 1️⃣1️⃣ Add Custom Furniture
1. Click **"Add furniture"** button (bottom left)
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**: What you want to call it
   - **Category**: Pick a category (Seating, Table, Bedroom, etc.)
   - **Width & Depth**: Size in meters
   - **Color**: Use the color picker
3. Click **"Add Furniture"**
4. Your custom furniture now appears in the left sidebar!

### 1️⃣2️⃣ Clear Everything
Click **"Clear room"** to remove all furniture at once

## Status Bar Information

Bottom of screen shows:
- **Room dimensions**: e.g., "5.0 × 4.0 m"
- **Item count**: How many pieces are placed
- **Status**: "Saved" = connected to database, "Offline" = working locally
- **Selected item info** (when you click furniture):
  - Item name
  - Rotation angle
  - Delete button

## Tips & Tricks

✓ **Drag from sidebar directly onto canvas** - don't click buttons, just drag!
✓ **Hold right-click and drag** in 3D view to orbit around and see furniture from different angles
✓ **Middle-click drag** in Plan view to pan around
✓ **Scroll wheel** to zoom in/out
✓ **All your layouts save** - they persist in the database even after closing the page
✓ **Furniture dimensions** are shown under each item name in the sidebar
✓ **Grid helps alignment** - use the grid in Plan view to see spacing clearly
